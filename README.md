# Legal Settlement Fund Distribution Reconciliation

Calculate and reconcile claimant distributions, liens, fees, returned payments, and residual settlement funds.

**Primary buyer:** Claims administrators, law firms, and settlement trustees. **Evidence:** settlement agreements, claimant eligibility, claim forms, damages, allocation formulas, liens, fees, taxes, distributions, returned payments, and residual funds.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Settlement term library
- Claimant identity registry
- Eligibility validation
- Claim form ingestion
- Damage category calculation
- Pro-rata allocation
- Lien holdback control
- Attorney fee allocation
- Administration expense
- Tax withholding control
- Distribution file generation
- Returned payment workflow
- Residual fund calculation
- Bank reconciliation
- Settlement claimant analytics
